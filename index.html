Undergraduate Researcher · University of California, Los Angeles

Hi! I’m Yubei Li, an undergraduate student in Statistics and Data Science at UCLA and an undergraduate researcher working with Prof. Ying Nian Wu and Prof. Kai-Wei Chang.

I am interested in LLM agents and multi-agent systems, particularly how agents can adapt memory, communication, and coordination for efficient and reliable long-horizon reasoning. My current work explores agent memory, adaptive communication, and multi-agent topology design.
